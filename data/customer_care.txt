Q: What is the Torrent Power customer care number?
A: You can reach Torrent Power customer support at 079-2255 1912 or the alternate number 079-6655 1912. For power-related complaints, a 24x7 number is available: 90999 91912.

Q: How do I contact Torrent Power for help?
A: You can call customer support at 079-2255 1912, use WhatsApp support at 6356 070 070, or reach out through the Torrent Power Connect app or customer portal.

Q: Does Torrent Power have WhatsApp support?
A: Yes, Torrent Power offers WhatsApp support at 6356 070 070 for quick assistance and queries.

Q: How do I register a complaint with Torrent Power?
A: Log into the Torrent Power customer portal or app, select "Register a Complaint" under the Complaints section, choose your issue type (billing, meter, outage, etc.), and submit the form. You can also call customer care directly.

Q: What if my complaint is not resolved by Torrent Power?
A: If unresolved, escalate to the Consumer Grievance Redressal Forum (CGRF) at 079-2749 2222 (extension 5940) or email consumerforum@torrentpower.com. Beyond that, you can approach the Electricity Ombudsman for your state.

Q: Does Torrent Power have a mobile app?
A: Yes, the official app is called "Torrent Power Connect," available on both iOS and Android, for bill payment, complaint registration, and account management.

Q: How do I report a power outage?
A: Report outages via the 24x7 helpline (90999 91912), the Torrent Power Connect app, or the customer portal's outage reporting section.

Q: What payment methods are available for my bill?
A: Torrent Power's customer portal and app offer multiple payment options for electricity bills, including online payment methods, alongside offline payment at authorized centers.
