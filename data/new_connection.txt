Q: How do I apply for a new electricity connection with Torrent Power?
A: You apply online through the Torrent Power customer portal — fill in personal details, address, and connection type, then upload required documents and pay the applicable fee online.

Q: How can I establish or set up a new electricity connection?
A: To establish a new connection, apply through the Torrent Power customer portal with your details and required documents. This is the same process as applying for a new connection.

Q: What documents are required for a new connection?
A: Typically identity proof, address proof, ownership or property documents, and a completed requisition form (Low Tension or High Tension form depending on load) are required.

Q: What is the difference between LT and HT connections?
A: LT (Low Tension) connections are for regular domestic/small commercial use, while HT (High Tension) connections are for larger industrial or commercial loads requiring a separate requisition form and process.

Q: How can I check my new connection application status?
A: After submitting your application, you receive a confirmation, and you can track your application status through the Torrent Power customer portal.

Q: How do I transfer a connection to my name after buying a property?
A: You submit a name transfer (NAAM transfer) request with ID proof, property proof (sale deed), and your latest electricity bill at the Torrent Power office or portal. It typically takes 7-21 working days depending on verification.

Q: What happens to the security deposit when I transfer a connection to a new name?
A: The existing security deposit on the service account, including any differential amount already paid, gets transferred to the new name holder's account as part of the name transfer process.

Q: How do I increase or decrease my sanctioned electricity load?
A: You can apply for load enhancement or load reduction through the Torrent Power portal or office using the relevant load extension/reduction form for your connection type (LT or HT).
