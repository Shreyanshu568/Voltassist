Q: What is the electricity tariff structure for Torrent Power domestic consumers?
A: Torrent Power's domestic (RGP) tariff is slab-based, approved by GERC (Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission). Rates start around ₹3.20 per unit for the lowest slab and progressively increase up to ₹5.00 per unit for higher consumption slabs.

Q: How much do I pay per unit of electricity?
A: Domestic consumers pay a slab-based rate approved by GERC, starting around ₹3.20 per unit and rising up to ₹5.00 per unit as consumption increases.

Q: What other charges are added to my electricity bill besides the per-unit rate?
A: Along with energy charges (slab-wise), your bill includes fixed charges based on your connection's sanctioned load, electricity duty and taxes, and meter rent if you're using a Torrent Power-owned meter.

Q: Who regulates Torrent Power's electricity tariff rates?
A: The Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (GERC) reviews and approves all tariff rates proposed by Torrent Power.

Q: Are there any subsidies or rebates available on my bill?
A: BPL households may get concessional rates on initial units, and senior citizens may be eligible for a rebate under residential billing policy, subject to Torrent Power's current schemes.

Q: Does Torrent Power serve areas outside Gujarat too?
A: Yes, besides Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar, and Surat in Gujarat, Torrent Power also distributes power in parts of Mumbai Metropolitan Region (Bhiwandi, Mumbra, Kalwa) in Maharashtra, Dahej, Dholera, Dadra & Nagar Haveli, Diu-Daman, and Agra in Uttar Pradesh.
