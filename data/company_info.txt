Q: What kind of company is Torrent Power?
A: Torrent Power is a leading integrated power utility company under the Torrent Group, involved in electricity generation, transmission, and distribution across India, along with gas and healthcare interests under the wider group.

Q: What is Torrent Power's total power generation capacity?
A: Torrent Power has an operational generation capacity of approximately 6,494 MW, including gas-based, coal-based, solar, and wind assets, plus a growing renewable portfolio of over 2,000 MW.

Q: What are Torrent Power's major power plants?
A: Key plants include SUGEN (1,530 MW), DGEN (1,200 MW), AMGEN (362 MW), and NABHA (1,400 MW).

Q: Which areas does Torrent Power supply electricity to?
A: Torrent Power distributes power in Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar, and Surat in Gujarat; parts of the Mumbai Metropolitan Region including Bhiwandi, Mumbra, and Kalwa in Maharashtra; Dahej, Dholera, Dadra & Nagar Haveli, Diu-Daman; and Agra in Uttar Pradesh.

Q: How does Torrent Power perform on service quality?
A: Torrent Power's licensed distribution areas in Gujarat are recognized for operational excellence, maintaining among the lowest AT&C (Aggregate Technical & Commercial) losses and best reliability indices in the country.
